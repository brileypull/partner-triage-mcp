# Partner-ops notes

## Partner program
Companies apply to become partners. Approved partners get a tier
and a partner manager.
"Hello, we're a 200 person M&E Consultancy in LA. We are interested in your partner program, we heard there are benefits and would like to get involved. What steps do we need to take to get started?"

## Tiers
Levels such as Registered, Silver and Gold. Higher tiers get bigger
discounts and more support, and usually must meet targets like
revenue or a number of certified people.
"Hello we are currently in the silver tier and would like to know what it takes to level up to the next tier, can you provide some information?"

## Partner manager
The person who owns the relationship with a partner. Also called a
channel manager.
"Hey this is acme studios, can you put us in touch with our person of contact, we recently were approved to join the program"

## Deal registration
A partner tells us about a customer they're working with. If approved,
they get protection for a set period, often around 90 days, so other
partners can't claim that customer.
"Hey we just started working with nike, can you make sure we have exclusive rights to work with them?"

## Deal conflict
Two partners register the same customer. A deal desk reviews it.
It's confidential: we never tell one partner who else registered.
"We at Joy Juice Consulting were surprised to hear that our customer Organic Farms is already registered with another partner. We thought we had it locked in since last month. Can you look into this?"

## Certification
Partner staff take training and exams. Tiers often require a minimum
number of certified people, and certifications expire.
"we want our engineers to become certified, what does that entail and how long are the certs valid for?"

## Co-marketing funds (MDF)
Money we give partners to co-fund marketing, like a joint webinar.
Partners request it with a plan; partner marketing approves it.
"We're planning a webinar on our agentic workflows. Does your program help cover the cost of promoting it?"
